## How to play charades

Charades is a guessing game where one player acts out a word or phrase without speaking while their team guesses.

1. Split into two teams.
2. A player picks a prompt and has 60 seconds to act it out silently.
3. Their team shouts guesses. A correct guess earns 1 point.
4. Teams alternate until everyone has had a turn. Highest score wins.

**Standard charades signals:**

- Hold up fingers for the number of words.
- Tap your forearm with fingers to show the number of syllables.
- Crank an old movie camera for a movie, open your palms for a book, and mimic singing for a song.
- Tug your ear for "sounds like."

## Charades FAQ

### What are good charades ideas?

Good charades ideas are easy to recognize and act out without props: animals (elephant, penguin), actions (taking a selfie, driving in traffic), jobs (chef, magician), and famous movies (Jaws, Titanic). Mix easy and hard prompts so everyone can play.

### What are funny charades ideas for adults?

Relatable everyday disasters work best: stepping on a LEGO, walking into a glass door, a bad first date, or waving back at someone who wasn't waving at you.

### What are easy charades ideas for kids?

Pick simple actions and animals: brushing teeth, riding a bike, flying a kite, monkey, frog, or kangaroo. Avoid prompts that need reading or abstract thinking.

### How many people do you need for charades?

You can play with as few as 3 people, but charades works best with 4 or more split into two teams.
