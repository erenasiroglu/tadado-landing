## The 15 best Christmas party games

### 1. Tadado: Forbidden Words

**Players:** 4+ in two teams · **Time:** 15–30 min · **Needs:** a phone

[Tadado](https://tadado.app/en) is a free party game app, and [Forbidden Words](/en/modes/forbidden-words) is its Taboo-style mode. Describe "Rudolph" without saying reindeer, red, nose, or Santa, and watch your team panic.

1. Split into two teams.
2. The describer gets 60 seconds per turn.
3. Correct guesses score; forbidden words cost a point.
4. Multipliers from ×2 to ×5 keep comebacks possible until the last round.

**Christmas tip:** Create an AI deck called "Christmas at our house" with your family's traditions and inside jokes.

### 2. Heads Up

**Players:** 3+ · **Time:** 10–20 min · **Needs:** a phone

The guesser holds the phone to their forehead while everyone shouts clues, sings, or acts. Perfect between dinner and dessert. Tadado's [Heads Up](/en/modes/heads-up) works offline, so it is ready even at grandma's house with no Wi-Fi.

### 3. Imposter

**Players:** 4–12 · **Time:** 15–30 min · **Needs:** a phone

Everyone sees the same secret word, like "Mistletoe," except the imposter. Each player gives a one-word clue, then the group votes. The imposter wins by blending in. Try [Tadado's Imposter mode](/en/modes/imposter) for a ready-made version.

### 4. White Elephant

**Players:** 6–30 · **Time:** 30–60 min · **Needs:** one wrapped gift per person

Players draw numbers, then take turns unwrapping a new gift or stealing one already opened. Set a price limit (like $20) and a steal limit of three per gift. It is the classic [office party gift exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_elephant_gift_exchange) for a reason.

### 5. Christmas Charades

**Players:** 4+ · **Time:** 20–30 min · **Needs:** nothing

Act out holiday prompts in silence: "wrapping a gift badly," "Santa stuck in a chimney," "untangling Christmas lights." Works for every age at the table.

### 6. Guess the Christmas Movie

**Players:** 4+ · **Time:** 15–20 min · **Needs:** a phone or paper

Describe a holiday movie using only emojis or a three-word plot summary. "Boy, home, burglars" is easier than you would think. First to guess earns a point.

### 7. Pass the Present

**Players:** 5–15 · **Time:** 15 min · **Needs:** a gift wrapped in many layers, music

Pass the gift around while music plays. When the music stops, whoever holds it removes one layer. A small treat inside each layer keeps kids hooked.

### 8. Ugly Sweater Contest

**Players:** 6+ · **Time:** 15 min · **Needs:** sweaters

Everyone walks the "runway," then votes in categories like Ugliest, Most Creative, and Best DIY. Pair it with a party game so the fun keeps going after the vote.

### 9. Santa Says

**Players:** 4+ kids · **Time:** 10 min · **Needs:** nothing

Simon Says with a festive twist: "Santa says hop like a reindeer." Ideal for burning off sugar before bedtime.

### 10. Christmas Would You Rather

**Players:** any · **Time:** 10–20 min · **Needs:** nothing

- Would you rather wrap 100 gifts or bake 500 cookies?
- Would you rather have a real tree that sheds or a fake one that looks fake?
- Would you rather open all your gifts on Christmas Eve or wait until noon on Christmas Day?

### 11. Holiday Trivia

**Players:** 4+ in teams · **Time:** 20–30 min · **Needs:** a phone or printout

Mix questions about traditions around the world, classic movies, and your own family. Winning team picks the first movie of the night.

### 12. Christmas Telephone Pictionary

**Players:** 4–12 · **Time:** 20 min · **Needs:** paper and pens

Alternate writing and drawing down the line. "Elf on the Shelf" turning into "a goblin in a cabinet" is guaranteed.

### 13. Two Truths and a Lie: Holiday Edition

**Players:** 3+ · **Time:** 15 min · **Needs:** nothing

Share three holiday memories, one of them fake. A gentle icebreaker for office parties and new partners meeting the family.

### 14. Snowball Fight Icebreaker

**Players:** 8+ · **Time:** 10–15 min · **Needs:** paper and pens

Everyone writes a fun fact or question on paper, crumples it, and throws it across the room. Each person grabs a "snowball" and guesses who wrote it or answers the question.

### 15. Christmas Song Association

**Players:** 3+ · **Time:** 10–15 min · **Needs:** nothing

Call out a word like "snow," "bells," or "home." The next player has five seconds to sing a line from any song that includes it. Holiday songs earn double points.

## Christmas party games FAQ

### What are the best Christmas party games for adults?

Tadado Forbidden Words, Imposter, White Elephant, and Christmas Song Association are top picks for adults. They mix teamwork, bluffing, and a little friendly sabotage.

### What Christmas games can the whole family play?

Christmas Charades, Heads Up, Pass the Present, and Holiday Trivia work for kids and grandparents alike. Rules are simple and nobody needs special skills.

### What are good games for an office Christmas party?

White Elephant, Two Truths and a Lie, the Snowball Fight Icebreaker, and team-based Forbidden Words are safe, inclusive, and easy to run for 10–30 coworkers.

### What Christmas games need no supplies?

Charades, Would You Rather, Santa Says, Two Truths and a Lie, and Song Association need nothing. A single phone with Tadado adds three more games.
