## How to play the Taboo word game

1. Split into two teams and sit alternating around the circle.
2. One player draws a card and describes the main word to their team without saying it or any forbidden word.
3. They have 60 seconds to get through as many cards as possible.
4. A correct guess scores 1 point. Saying a forbidden word, a part of it, or using gestures costs 1 point.
5. An opposing player watches the card and buzzes violations.
6. Teams alternate until a set score (like 30 points) or number of rounds.

## Tips for making your own Taboo cards

- Choose forbidden words people would naturally say first. If "pizza" does not block "cheese," it is too easy.
- Keep 4–5 forbidden words per card for adults, 3–4 for kids.
- Theme a deck for your group: inside jokes, a holiday, or a TV show you all watch.
- Or skip the cardboard: [Tadado's AI decks](/en/blog/create-ai-word-game-deck) generate Forbidden Words cards on any topic in seconds.

## Taboo words FAQ

### What are good Taboo words?

Good Taboo words are familiar nouns or ideas with obvious clues you are not allowed to use, like Pizza (cheese, slice, Italian, pepperoni) or Beach (sand, ocean, sun, swim). The challenge comes from the forbidden words, not from obscure vocabulary.

### How many forbidden words are on a Taboo card?

Most Taboo-style cards have 5 forbidden words. Using 4 makes the game a bit easier, which works well for casual groups and kids.

### What are hard Taboo words?

Abstract concepts are the hardest: procrastinate, nostalgia, déjà vu, paradox, and inflation. They have no physical shape to describe, so players have to get creative.

### How many players do you need for Taboo?

At least 4 players in two teams of 2. The game gets better with 6–10 players.
