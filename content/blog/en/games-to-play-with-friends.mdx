The best **games to play with friends** are fast to learn, need no supplies, and get everyone talking: word games like [Tadado Forbidden Words](/en/modes/forbidden-words) and [Heads Up](/en/modes/heads-up), bluffing games like [Imposter](/en/modes/imposter) and Mafia, and classics like Charades and Two Truths and a Lie. Here are **20 games** for any group size, mood, or setting, with quick rules for each.

**Tadado works for pairs too:** Forbidden Words and Heads Up are great with **two people** (one gives clues, one guesses). Imposter needs at least three players for a real vote. One phone runs all three modes.

## The 20 best games to play with friends

### 1. Tadado: Forbidden Words

[Tadado](https://tadado.app/en) is a free party game app, and Forbidden Words is its Taboo-style mode. Describe a word to your team without saying it or the forbidden words under it. Fast rounds, team scores, and ×2 to ×5 multipliers make it the easiest way to get a whole group laughing. With two people, take turns describing and guessing on one phone. See our [Taboo words list](/en/blog/taboo-words-list) for 100 free cards.

### 2. Heads Up

One player holds the phone to their forehead while friends give clues, act, or hum until they guess. It is ideal when the group is tired or sitting down. Works with **two players** as well. Need prompts? Use our [250+ charades ideas](/en/blog/charades-ideas).

### 3. Imposter

Everyone gets the same secret word except one person, the imposter. Each player gives a one-word clue, then the group votes on who is bluffing. Rounds take three minutes and the arguments last longer. Get started with our [Imposter game words](/en/blog/imposter-game-words).

### 4. Charades

Act out a word or phrase in silence while your team guesses. Works for every age and needs nothing at all.

### 5. Mafia / Werewolf

A secret group of "mafia" eliminates players each night while the town tries to unmask them by day. Mafia shines with 8–15 players.

### 6. Two Truths and a Lie

Share three facts about yourself, one of them false. Everyone guesses the lie. The best icebreaker for new friend groups.

### 7. Fishbowl

Everyone writes names or phrases on slips. Play three rounds with the same slips: describe freely, use only one word, then act it out. Jokes build with every round.

### 8. Most Likely To

Read a prompt like "Who's most likely to become famous?" and everyone points at someone on the count of three.

### 9. Would You Rather

Ask impossible choices and defend your answer. Works anywhere: on a road trip, at dinner, or while waiting in line.

### 10. Never Have I Ever

Each player says something they have never done. Anyone who has done it puts a finger down. Last person with fingers up wins.

### 11. Who Am I?

Stick a famous name on each player's forehead with a sticky note. Ask yes-or-no questions to figure out who you are.

### 12. 20 Questions

One person thinks of something. Everyone else gets 20 yes-or-no questions to guess it. Perfect for two people or a small group.

### 13. Telephone Pictionary

Alternate between writing a phrase and drawing the previous one down the line. Reveal the hilarious transformation at the end.

### 14. Contact

One player thinks of a word and reveals its first letter. Others try to guess words starting with those letters, and if two players "make contact" on the same word, the next letter is revealed.

### 15. Song Association

Call out a word. The next player has five seconds to sing a song lyric that includes it, or they are out.

### 16. Categories

Pick a category like "pizza toppings" and go around the circle naming one each. Hesitate or repeat and you are out.

### 17. Hot Seat

One player answers rapid-fire questions from the group for 60 seconds. Skips are allowed, but they are suspicious.

### 18. Psychiatrist

One player leaves the room while the group picks a secret rule (like "answer as the person to your left"). The psychiatrist returns and asks questions until they crack the rule.

### 19. Word Association

Say a word; the next player instantly says the first related word that comes to mind. Pause or repeat and you are out.

### 20. The Floor Is Lava

Shout "The floor is lava!" and everyone has five seconds to get off the ground. A silly classic for groups with energy to burn.

## Games to play with friends FAQ

### What are fun games to play with friends in person?

The most fun in-person games are Tadado Forbidden Words, Heads Up, Imposter, Charades, Mafia, and Two Truths and a Lie. They are quick to learn and get the whole group involved.

### What games can you play with friends with no supplies?

Charades, Mafia, Two Truths and a Lie, Would You Rather, Never Have I Ever, 20 Questions, Contact, Categories, and Psychiatrist need nothing at all.

### What are good games to play when bored with friends?

Try Imposter for a quick bluffing round, Song Association for laughs, or Heads Up when nobody wants to get up from the couch.

### Can you play Tadado with two people?

Yes. Forbidden Words and Heads Up work well with two people on one phone: one player gives clues, the other guesses, then swap. Imposter needs at least three players so the vote still means something.
