Below are 12 Halloween games for adults, teens and mixed groups, plus how to play most of them on one phone.

## Quick answer: the best Halloween games for friends

1. **Spooky Taboo:** describe Halloween words without saying the forbidden ones.
2. **Horror-movie Heads Up:** phone on your forehead, friends act out monsters and movie villains.
3. **Costume charades:** guess who everyone dressed up as, without words.
4. **Scary story chain:** each player adds one sentence to a story that gets weirder every round.
5. **Two truths and a lie, haunted edition:** two real spooky experiences and one invented one.
6. **Monster mash quiz:** teams race to name vampires, witches and zombies from pop culture.
7. **Pumpkin face-off:** the fastest team to carve or draw a pumpkin face wins.
8. **Murder mystery round:** one secret "ghost" tries to eliminate players with a wink.
9. **Mummy wrap race:** wrap a teammate in toilet paper before the timer runs out.
10. **Guess the sound:** play creepy sound effects and guess what they are.
11. **Halloween Pictionary:** draw bats, cauldrons and haunted houses against the clock.
12. **Custom AI deck about your group:** a deck of Halloween words built around your own inside jokes.

## 1. Spooky Taboo (Forbidden Words)

Split into two teams. One player describes a word like *VAMPIRE* or *HAUNTED HOUSE* while avoiding the forbidden words on the card, such as *blood*, *bite* or *night*. It is the classic Taboo party game with a Halloween twist, and it works for 4 to 20 players.

## 2. Horror-movie Heads Up

Hold the phone on your forehead while your friends shout clues, hum theme songs or act out famous horror villains. Tilt down when you get it right and tilt up to pass. It is loud, fast and great for photos.

**Tip:** Tadado's Cinema deck already covers movies and series, so it is an easy warm-up before your custom Halloween round.

## 3. Costume charades

Everyone writes their costume on a slip of paper. Players draw a slip and act it out with no words while the room guesses. Bonus points if the costume owner has to guess their own.

## 4. Scary story chain

Sit in a circle with the lights low. One person starts a scary story with a single sentence, and each player adds the next line. The person who makes the group laugh instead of shiver loses a point.

## 5. Two truths and a lie, haunted edition

Each player shares two true spooky or strange experiences and one made-up one. The group votes on the lie. It is the perfect icebreaker for Halloween parties where not everyone knows each other.

## 6. Monster mash quiz

Teams get 60 seconds to name as many monsters, witches or horror villains from movies, books and games as they can. No repeats allowed.

## 7. to 11. Quick physical games

- **Pumpkin face-off:** teams carve or draw a pumpkin face in five minutes. The group votes on the winner.
- **Murder mystery wink:** one secret "ghost" eliminates players by winking at them. Everyone else tries to catch the ghost.
- **Mummy wrap race:** one roll of toilet paper per team. Fastest full mummy wins.
- **Guess the sound:** play door creaks, howls and thunder from a speaker and guess each one.
- **Halloween Pictionary:** draw spooky words against the clock on paper or a whiteboard.
